I’m Marco Túlio Quintino, an associate professor (maître de conférences [HDR]) in computer science at [Sorbonne University](https://sciences.sorbonne-universite.fr/) in Paris. I am a member of the [Quantum Information team](https://qi.lip6.fr/) at [LIP6](https://www.lip6.fr/recherche/team_membres.php?acronyme=QI), a joint research institute of Sorbonne University and [CNRS](https://www.cnrs.fr/). I also serve as co-head (responsable adjoint) of Sorbonne University’s [Master’s programme in Quantum Information](https://sciences.sorbonne-universite.fr/formation-sciences/masters/master-informatique/parcours-IQ).

My research focuses on quantum information and quantum computing. In particular, quantum correlations (entanglement, Bell nonlocality, EPR steering, measurement incompatibility, prepare-and-measure scenarios), quantum state and channel discrimination, higher-order quantum operations, causality in quantum theory, and semidefinite programming.

All my scientific papers are available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/a/quintino_m_1.html), and citation statistics can be found on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=9S-Jrs4AAAAJ). I use [GitHub](https://github.com/mtcq) to share computational tools and code developed for my research.

Interested in learning more about quantum information and topics related to my research? Please check the [resources page]({{ '/resources/' | relative_url }}).
